Hi Thomas, thank you for reporting this. Error AW-ERR-4012 indicates a checksum mismatch during the delta update process. This is a known issue affecting sensors with firmware versions below 3.1.8.

Workaround: Please perform a full firmware flash instead of delta update.
1. SSH into the gateway at 192.168.1.100
2. Run: acme-cli flash --full --target AW-DE-0108

We are releasing a patch next week. Ticket ID: SUP-2026-04821, Priority: P1.
