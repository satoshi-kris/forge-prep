Employee Onboarding Guide — Acme Corp

Welcome to Acme Corp! This guide will help you get started with our tools, processes, and culture.

Chapter 1: Getting Started
Your first week at Acme will include orientation sessions, team introductions, and access setup. Please bring your government-issued ID and any relevant certifications.

Chapter 2: Tools and Systems
We use Jira for project tracking, Confluence for documentation, and Slack for daily communication. All code is managed through GitLab, and deployments are handled via our internal CI/CD pipeline called AcmeDeploy.

Chapter 3: Security Policies
All employees must complete security training within their first two weeks. Never share your credentials. Report any suspicious activity to security@acmecorp.com. For emergencies, call +33 1 42 68 53 00.

Chapter 4: Benefits
Contact HR at hr@acmecorp.com or visit the internal portal. Your employee number (format: ACM-XXXXX) is required for all benefit requests.
