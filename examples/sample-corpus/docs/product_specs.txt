Product Specification: AcmeWidget v3.2

1. Overview
AcmeWidget is our flagship IoT sensor platform deployed across 2,400+ manufacturing facilities in Europe. The v3.2 release adds predictive maintenance capabilities powered by on-device ML inference.

2. Technical Architecture
Edge Processing: ARM Cortex-M7 at 480MHz with 1MB SRAM
Connectivity: LoRaWAN, NB-IoT, WiFi 6
Inference Engine: TensorFlow Lite Micro (quantized INT8)
Data Pipeline: MQTT to Kafka to Snowflake with dbt transformations

3. ML Model Specifications
The predictive maintenance model uses a 1D CNN trained on 18 months of vibration data from 340 facilities. Current accuracy: 94.2 percent (precision: 91.8, recall: 96.1). Model size: 142KB quantized.

4. Deployment Schedule
Beta: March 2026 at 50 facilities in France and Germany
General Availability: June 2026 for all EU facilities
